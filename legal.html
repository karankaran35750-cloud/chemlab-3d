Legal Policies, Privacy Disclosures & Laboratory Safety Standards

Effective Date: November 2026 • Applicable across all ChemVerse 3D educational portals and simulation engines.

1. Privacy Policy & Advertising Disclosures

ChemVerse 3D respects user privacy and adheres to strict data-minimization standards. We do not collect, sell, or store personally identifiable information (PII) from students or educators browsing our virtual laboratory.

  • Google AdSense & Automated Advertising: On informational and educational content pages (Overview, Encyclopedia, Instruments, Periodic Table, and Legal pages), third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to ChemVerse 3D and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Ad-Free Simulation Workspace: The dedicated interactive 3D Virtual Chemistry Workbench (lab.html) is maintained strictly free of third-party advertisements to guarantee an unobstructed academic workspace.
  • Security & Credential Isolation: All external AI synthesis requests are routed through encrypted server-side endpoints. No private API tokens or secret keys are ever stored in client-side source code or browser local storage.

2. Terms of Service & Academic Usage

By accessing ChemVerse 3D, you agree to use the platform solely for lawful educational, research, and computational visualization purposes.

  • Educational Reference: Reaction equations, enthalpy values ($\Delta H^\circ$), and qualitative salt analysis procedures are modeled on standard IUPAC conventions and Class XI / XII NCERT curricula.
  • Intellectual Property: All procedural 3D glassware shaders, stoichiometry engine rules, and user interface layouts are the intellectual property of ChemVerse 3D.

3. Unrestricted Simulation Sandbox & Physical Lab Safety Disclaimer

ChemVerse 3D provides an unrestricted computational sandbox allowing students and researchers to simulate any combination of reagents, temperatures, and pressures—including vigorous exothermic reactions (such as alkali metals in water) and reactions evolving toxic gases ($\text{Cl}_2, \text{NO}_2, \text{H}_2\text{S}, \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{NC}$).

  • Mandatory GHS Hazard Pop-Ups: Whenever an unrestricted reaction generates hazardous conditions, ChemVerse 3D displays a formal Globally Harmonized System (GHS) Hazard Advisory detailing the real-world risks.
  • Physical Laboratory Caution: Never attempt high-energy, concentrated-acid, or toxic-gas reactions outside a certified chemical laboratory equipped with a laminar-flow Fume Hood, blast shield, eye-wash station, and direct supervision by a qualified chemistry instructor.
